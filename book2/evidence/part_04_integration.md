## Relation Audit

- **alignment** uses exact source, capability, and interface contracts
- **containment** records parent-child ownership and selected inspection scope
- **execution** records ordered value-producing stages and first-failure non-execution
- **limits** record typed acceptance, count, collision, policy, and numerical-difference results

No chapter substitutes one relation for another. Chapter 14 explicitly denies that zoom order is runtime order. Chapter 15 uses arrows and concrete values only when runtime order begins. Chapter 16 keeps heterogeneous result types separate rather than placing them on one invented scale.

## Terminology Audit

- **identity** remains the stable architecture or instance identifier, not similarity of labels
- **contract** remains a declared set of accepted interfaces and requirements
- **scale** remains inspection scope, not execution depth or explanatory completeness
- **runtime order** remains the sequence in which concrete fixture values are validated and produced
- **work count** remains a declared structural arithmetic or allocation inventory, not elapsed time
- **context capacity** remains a fixture admission boundary, not useful production context
- **representation loss** remains the demonstrated many-to-one unknown-token collision in the fixed vocabulary
- **decoding policy** remains an explicit selection rule over fixed logits
- **component contribution** remains a controlled numerical difference, not semantic attribution
- **outside operational evidence** classifies claim scope and does not establish a predicate as false

## Boundary Audit

Part IV does not claim:

- that aligned disciplines or their artifacts are identical
- that one attention row explains a block, model, reasoning process, or semantic result
- that containment order is execution order
- that operation or allocation counts imply latency, throughput, hardware use, or energy
- that the fixed decoded token demonstrates language quality or understanding
- that fixture context, width, vocabulary, or policy boundaries generalize to production models
- that one component-removal result proves universal causal importance
- that fluent output establishes personhood, authorship, interpretation, or understanding
- that failure to establish those predicates proves they are impossible
