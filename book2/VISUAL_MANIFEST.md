## Progression

The anchors form a deliberate sequence:

1. Chapters 1–5 expose formal and executable structure.
2. Chapters 6–9 make learning geometry and machine cost visible.
3. Chapters 10–12 show attention becoming an implemented architecture.
4. Chapters 13–16 integrate the lineages, trace execution, and measure constraints.

No anchor may migrate between chapters without updating both this manifest and the affected chapter rationale.
