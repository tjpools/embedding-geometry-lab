## Framing comments

- The distinction **"transformers as individuals / LLMs as culture"** is promising, but it should be presented as a **heuristic analogy**, not as a literal identity claim.
- The strongest parts of the idea are the structural parallels:
  - local context vs. global coherence
  - bounded inference vs. accumulated tradition
  - execution vs. cultural memory
- The assembly-language angle is compelling because it links **intent, instruction, and execution** in one artifact.
- The phrase **`sub rsp, 28h  ; human intent`** is memorable and could work as an epigraph or section heading.
