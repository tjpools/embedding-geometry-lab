## Purpose

Send a short, polished excerpt that presents the architecture of the book without sending the full manuscript.

## Excerpt package structure

1. Title page  
   **Working Manuscript Excerpt**  
   *from a book in progress*

2. Framing note  
   This excerpt presents the core architectural framework of the book. It introduces the Three Ages as an inheritance chain and sketches the conceptual structure that organizes the larger manuscript. At this stage, I’m most interested in whether the inheritance framing feels clear, coherent, and strong enough to carry the project.

3. Main content  
   - **The Three Ages as an Inheritance Chain**
   - **The Assembly Practitioner as the Hinge Figure**

4. Closing note  
   I’d welcome your sense of whether the inheritance framing feels like a strong organizing principle, and whether the hinge figure helps make the architecture concrete.

## Why this package works

- It lets readers evaluate the conceptual spine without getting lost in draft prose.
- It protects the full manuscript while still inviting serious engagement.
- It signals that the project is structurally mature.
