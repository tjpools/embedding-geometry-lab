# KDP Preorder Rules and Launch Gates

**Verified:** August 12, 2026  
**Scope:** Kindle eBook preorder guidance from Amazon KDP Help

This document corrects an unsafe assumption: a placeholder manuscript is not sufficient to submit a live Kindle preorder under the current KDP instructions.

## Verified Rules

Amazon currently states:

- Kindle eBooks may be scheduled for preorder up to one year in advance.
- A title may remain privately editable in **Pre-order Draft** status.
- To submit the preorder, the publisher must upload the **final manuscript file** containing the version intended for customers.
- The preorder remains a draft until **Submit for pre-order** is selected.
- The final manuscript and preorder submission must occur before the KDP deadline; Amazon's setup guidance says at least 72 hours before that deadline.
- Missing the submission deadline cancels the preorder, locks further editing of that title, and blocks new preorders for one year.
- Cancelling a live preorder also blocks new preorders for one year.
- One catalog-wide exclusion permits delaying a release by up to 30 days without penalty. Repeated delay carries preorder restrictions.
- A public Amazon detail page and preorder button follow submission and review; a private draft does not create the promised public identity anchor.
- Preorder is available for Kindle eBooks. Print books use scheduled release-date features rather than Kindle preorder.
- KDP permits up to three categories and up to seven keyword phrases. Category availability varies by format and marketplace.

## Operational Interpretation

The safe sequence for each book is:

1. Finalize the public metadata and cover.
2. Choose a conservative release date no more than one year out.
3. Create the title in KDP as **Pre-order Draft**.
4. Keep all public language at **forthcoming** while the title remains private.
5. Finish and validate the customer-ready manuscript.
6. Upload the final manuscript, preview the eBook, complete rights and pricing, and submit.
7. Wait for KDP review and confirm the live detail page.
8. Only then publish **available for preorder** language and the Amazon URL.

## Internal Safety Margin

The KDP deadline is a hard external boundary, not the production target. Set an internal manuscript freeze at least 14 days before the displayed KDP submission deadline. This provides time for EPUB validation, Kindle Previewer inspection, metadata review, correction, upload, and KDP review.

Book Two should pass this gate before Book Three unless both final manuscripts are independently ready. A failure on either preorder can affect the ability to operate the other.

## Submission Gate

Do not click **Submit for pre-order** unless all statements are true:

- the uploaded manuscript is the complete customer-delivery version
- the cover is final and rights-cleared
- title, subtitle, author, series, and edition data match the files exactly
- description contains no URLs, reviews, availability claims, pricing, or time-sensitive promotion
- categories are accurate in the current primary-marketplace selector
- keywords are relevant, non-promotional, and non-redundant
- territorial rights and pricing are confirmed
- the final production process has been reviewed for all required KDP disclosures
- the release can be met without relying on KDP's one-time delay exclusion
