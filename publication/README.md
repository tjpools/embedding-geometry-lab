# Books Two and Three Publication Package

**Status:** Drafted August 12, 2026  
**Author:** Terrence J McLaughlin
**Trilogy:** *The Geometry of Meaning*

This directory coordinates the public rollout of the trilogy's two forthcoming volumes while preserving the boundary between public identity and manuscript readiness.

## Public Arc

- **Book One — Tools:** structure is revealed
- **Book Two — Architecture:** structure becomes a machine
- **Book Three — Philosophy:** structure encounters interpretation

Book Two and Book Three should be prepared together and released in sequence. Their typography, author identity, series treatment, and announcement cadence should agree; their promises, categories, keywords, and descriptions should remain distinct.

## Package

- [KDP_RULES.md](KDP_RULES.md) records the verified preorder constraints and launch gates.
- [BOOK2_METADATA.md](BOOK2_METADATA.md) contains Book Two's metadata, descriptions, category intent, keywords, and back copy.
- [BOOK3_METADATA.md](BOOK3_METADATA.md) contains Book Three's corresponding package.
- [ANNOUNCEMENTS.md](ANNOUNCEMENTS.md) separates forthcoming-project language from live-preorder language.

## Shared Author Positioning

Terrence J McLaughlin writes about computation, tools, mathematical structure, and human collaboration with transformer models. His work joins readable inquiry to inspectable practice: conversation, building, testing, reverse engineering, and revision.

This positioning makes no unverified credential claim and preserves human authorship and responsibility.

## Unresolved Fields

Do not submit either title until these fields are resolved:

- exact series numbering and Book One linkage
- release dates and internal delivery dates
- list price, royalty option, territories, and KDP Select decision
- current category paths in the primary marketplace
- final cover files and thumbnail checks
- final manuscript files and Kindle Previewer checks
- required KDP disclosure based on the final content-production process

The metadata sheets use `[DECIDE]` and `[CONFIRM]` where a publishing decision or account-specific fact is still required.
