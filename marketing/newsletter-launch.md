## I’m excited to share a new book project

# *Embedding Geometry*
## *A Walkable Introduction to AI Through Building, Testing, and Collaboration*

This book offers a new way to understand AI: not as a rival mind or a cycle of hype, but as a tool you can touch. It traces a path from tools and symbols to computation, structure, and transformers, showing how understanding grows through building, testing, and collaboration.

Through stories, conceptual analysis, and structural insight, it asks what changes when thinking is no longer purely individual, authorship becomes collaborative, and knowledge moves through new environments.

At the center of the book is a simple claim:

**AI is easier to understand when the machinery stays visible.**

This is an orientation book, not a technical manual. It is for readers who want a serious, grounded first contact with what AI is changing not only in technology, but in thought itself.
