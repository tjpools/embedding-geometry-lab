## I’m excited to share a new book project

# *Embedding Geometry*
## *A Coordinate Atlas for Intelligence*

This book offers a new way to understand AI: not as a rival mind or a cycle of hype, but as a structure that can be walked. It traces a path from tools and symbols to computation, geometry, and transformers, showing how intelligence becomes distributed across people, models, devices, and institutions.

Through stories, conceptual analysis, and structural insight, it asks what changes when thinking is no longer purely individual, authorship becomes collaborative, and knowledge moves through new environments.

At the center of the book is a simple claim:

**Intelligence is no longer a possession. It is a place.**

This is a book for readers who want a serious, grounded account of what AI is changing not only in technology, but in thought itself.

---

## A short excerpt

> We are living inside a new configuration of intelligence. It is not contained in a single mind, a single machine, or a single model. It is distributed across people, tools, systems, and forms of representation.
>
> The question is not whether AI will replace human intelligence. The deeper question is what happens when human intelligence becomes part of a larger manifold of reasoning — when thinking becomes a joint performance across person, model, interface, and machine.
