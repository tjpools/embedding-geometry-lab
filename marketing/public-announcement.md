## Long Version

I, Terrence J McLaughlin, have published a project called *Embedding Geometry: The Manifold of Human and Machine Reasoning*.

At the center of it is a claim: AI becomes easier to understand when we stop treating it as magic, personality, or spectacle and start treating it as structure. This book traces that structure historically and technically, following the lineage from older tools and symbolic systems into computation, geometry, gradients, and transformers.

The aim is not to flatten the subject into slogans. The aim is to make it walkable.

That is why the project does not end at the manuscript. The book is the story layer. A short epilogue provides one concrete runtime proof that a simple question can open a deep corridor of understanding. The repository provides the structural layer around the book: builds, notes, artifacts, and executable traces. The metrics provide a self-describing layer: counts, heatmaps, and chapter-level measurements that show how the object tightened and changed under collaboration.

The result is not just a book about AI. It is a public cognitive instrument for readers who want to understand what AI is doing to thought, tools, and collaboration.
