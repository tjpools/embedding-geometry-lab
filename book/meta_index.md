## Structural interpretation

Taken together, these files form a meta-layer around the manuscript:

- presentation
- excerpting
- correspondence
- propagation
- state transition tracking
- reflection

This is not separate from the book’s thesis. It mirrors it. The manuscript is not only arguing for inheritance, executable culture, and the continuity of tool-making; it is beginning to enact those ideas through its own recorded construction and circulation.

Use this index as a starting point for navigating the book’s meta-structure: the layer in which the project documents not only what it says, but how it is being built, analyzed, and moved through the world.
