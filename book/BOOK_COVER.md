# Book Cover: Public Framing

![Stickman lifting a rock to reveal gold](cover.png)

The current cover concept remains the right one: a human figure lifts an opaque structural mass and reveals the intelligible units beneath it. The gesture is simple, legible, and structurally faithful to the book.

Its public meaning is straightforward:

- effort
- structure
- revelation

Its structural meaning is deeper:

- the human is the entering intelligence
- the lifted mass is obscured structure
- the revealed units are understanding made visible

This is why the image works. It does not decorate the thesis. It compresses it.

The cover should begin as a glyph and resolve into geometry. At first glance the reader sees a person, a rock, and a reveal. On closer inspection the reveal is not treasure or a robot. It is a field of structured components: discrete golden units that read as local pieces of a larger system. Each unit stands on its own, but together they orient a wider surface.

This is the book's governing relationship in visual form: orientation first, depth second; contact before mechanism.

## Design Intent

For Scribus and print packaging, the cover should read in this order:

1. title
2. emblem
3. subtitle
4. author

The concept should stay bold, geometric, and uncluttered. The title carries the intellectual weight. The emblem carries the symbolic action. The rest should support that hierarchy without noise.

The cover stance is invitation, not instruction. Walkable, not technical. It should signal accessibility while hinting at the underlying mathematical shape.
