### Description

Use this text:

`AI is not a mystery. It is a tool you can touch.`

`Embedding Geometry is a clear, walkable introduction to modern AI for readers who want understanding without hype. Rather than treating artificial intelligence as magic, Terrence J McLaughlin shows how tools, structure, notation, and collaboration make modern reasoning systems legible.`

`This book does not require a background in machine learning or advanced mathematics. It offers a serious entry point for curious readers, programmers, and independent learners who want to understand what AI is doing without surrendering to jargon.`

`Across eighteen chapters, the book moves through programming, assembly language, differentials, stories, geometry, and transformer collaboration. The result is not a sales pitch for AI. It is a structural introduction to how meaning, representation, and reasoning become visible in modern systems.`

`Inside, readers will find:`

- `a grounded introduction to AI for technically curious readers`
- `a human-scale path into transformers, embeddings, and reasoning tools`
- `a serious but accessible account of human-machine collaboration`

Do not hit publish until all of these are true:

1. The EPUB uploads cleanly
2. The preview is clean
3. The description reads well in KDP’s layout
4. The categories are close enough to the intended positioning
5. The seven keyword fields are filled
6. The price is intentionally set at `$8.99` or a consciously chosen alternative
