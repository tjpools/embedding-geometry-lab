# Book Three — Closure Probe Contract

**Status:** Canonical cross-cutting contract established August 14, 2026

Book Three tests closure as Book Two tested mechanism: through bounded, inspectable records that preserve the difference between operational evidence and warranted action. The philosophical operations that identify and examine that difference are governed by [PHILOSOPHY_CONTRACT.md](PHILOSOPHY_CONTRACT.md).

## Canonical Thesis

> Humans responsibly convert bounded operational evidence into warranted action by making values, judgment, responsibility, and revision visible.

Closure is an evidence-bounded commitment:

$$
(E, V, J, R) \rightarrow A
$$

- $E$ = bounded evidence
- $V$ = declared values
- $J$ = unresolved human judgment
- $R$ = responsibility and accountability
- $A$ = warranted action

The arrow is not a deductive entailment. It marks a transition that must be argued, authorized, observed, and revised. Evidence constrains closure but does not erase values, judgment, or responsibility.

## Required Record

Every chapter-level closure probe must identify:

1. **bounded evidence:** what was observed, how it was produced, and what its method does not establish
2. **unresolved judgment:** the question the evidence cannot decide by operation alone
3. **declared values:** the priorities, duties, tolerances, or rights used to evaluate possible actions
4. **accountable actor:** the person or institution authorized to decide and answer for the result
5. **authorized action:** the specific act, reliance, refusal, escalation, or delay selected
6. **consequences and revision:** what happened, what would disconfirm the judgment, and how the action can be corrected

## Evidence Classes

A probe must label each input as one or more of:

- inherited technical evidence
- primary or secondary historical evidence
- empirical evidence
- inspectable comparative case
- philosophical inference
- declared value judgment
- analogy
- open speculation

Classes may interact but may not silently substitute for one another. A generated justification or contextual summary is an artifact to inspect, not its own warrant.

## Probe Tests

A closure probe passes only when:

- the inherited evidence retains its original scope and limitations
- the unresolved judgment is stated rather than hidden inside technical language
- the values affecting the decision are explicit enough to contest
- authority and responsibility are attributable
- the action is concrete enough for consequences to be inspected
- a revision path or stopping rule exists
- plausible alternatives or counterexamples are considered
- the conclusion claims no more warrant than the complete record supports

## Failure Classes

- **EVIDENCE_SCOPE_EXCEEDED:** the conclusion outruns the inherited evidence
- **VALUE_INPUT_HIDDEN:** a normative choice is presented as a technical result
- **JUDGMENT_LAUNDERED:** human judgment is attributed to the model, metric, policy, or workflow
- **RESPONSIBILITY_UNASSIGNED:** no actor can answer for the authorized action
- **ACTION_UNSPECIFIED:** the argument recommends reliance without naming what will be done
- **REVISION_PATH_MISSING:** consequences cannot trigger correction, withdrawal, or escalation
- **AUTHORITY_CONFUSED_WITH_WARRANT:** delegated power is treated as proof that an action is justified
- **FLUENCY_CONFUSED_WITH_JUSTIFICATION:** a persuasive explanation is treated as verified ground

## Book Boundary

Book Two establishes what the bounded machine did and where its operational evidence stops. Book Three may inherit those findings but cannot strengthen them. It owns the visible transition from evidence to action and the responsibility for testing that transition.

The contract applies across every crate and chapter. It complements [PHILOSOPHY_CONTRACT.md](PHILOSOPHY_CONTRACT.md) and [OWNERSHIP_CONTRACT.md](OWNERSHIP_CONTRACT.md): the philosophy contract governs conceptual and normative inquiry; ownership governs attribution, provenance, and responsibility; the closure probe governs the conversion of evidence into action.
