## Boundary

Book Three asks how humans responsibly convert bounded operational evidence into warranted action while keeping values, judgment, authority, consequences, and revision visible. Derived meaning, closure, geometry, and lineage remain necessary fields, but none warrants action by itself.

Book Three does not carry Book Two's task of explaining the transformer architecture or reconstructing its AI, mathematical, and programming lineages. Those are dependencies of the philosophical argument, not substitutes for it.

The human and transformer are not presented as co-authors. Book Three examines their collaboration as an operative system while keeping authorship, verification, and responsibility human.
