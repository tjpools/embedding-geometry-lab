# Geometry

This crate tests geometric language as a model for possibility, inference, relation, and constraint.

Modules:

1. `manifolds` — local structure, global organization, and spaces of possibility
2. `trajectories` — paths through representations, interpretations, and inquiry
3. `non_commutativity` — why order changes reachable states and derived outcomes
4. `attention_geometry` — what geometric accounts of transformer attention reveal and conceal

Book Two supplies the relevant mathematical and architectural evidence. This crate cannot infer an ontology of meaning directly from embedding plots, attention patterns, or geometric vocabulary.

**Ownership invariants:** P3, P4, R2, R3, R4. Geometric models retain their technical scope, and visual or mathematical elegance cannot substitute for philosophical warrant under [the ownership contract](../OWNERSHIP_CONTRACT.md).

**Crate question:** How does geometry constrain what can be represented, reached, or interpreted?
